# Prior Authorization Policy

Riverbend Health Network (fictional) — Revenue Cycle Policy RC-201. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Purpose
This policy describes when staff must obtain prior authorization (PA) from a health plan before a service is scheduled, and how to handle urgent and retroactive requests. Missing authorization is the leading cause of avoidable denials (denial code CO-197).

## Services that commonly require prior authorization
Advanced imaging (MRI and CT), elective inpatient surgery including joint replacement, echocardiography under HMO and Medicaid plans, specialty drugs (formulary tier 5) and GLP-1 medications. The payer-specific rule table in the scheduling system is the source of truth for any individual CPT code and payer combination.

## Who is responsible
The ordering provider's office initiates the request. The central authorization team verifies submission, tracks status, and confirms approval before the scheduling team books the appointment. Schedulers must not book an elective service that requires PA until an authorization number is recorded on the order.

## Required documentation
Every request must include the ordering provider's NPI, ICD-10 diagnosis codes supporting medical necessity, the CPT code(s), the most recent relevant clinical note, and evidence of conservative therapy when the payer requires it. For lumbar spine MRI, commercial plans expect six weeks of documented conservative therapy unless red-flag symptoms are present (for example progressive neurological deficit, suspected infection, or malignancy).

## Turnaround times
Standard requests are decided by commercial and Medicare Advantage plans within 5 to 7 calendar days, and by Medicaid managed care within 14 calendar days. Urgent requests, where waiting could seriously jeopardize the patient's health, must be decided within 72 hours. Mark a request urgent only when the ordering provider attests to clinical urgency.

## Retroactive authorization
Retroactive authorization may be requested within 14 days of an emergency admission or an unplanned procedure. Elective services provided without authorization are not eligible for retro-authorization and the charges may not be billed to the patient for in-network commercial plans.

## Expiration
Approved authorizations are valid for 60 days from approval unless the payer states otherwise. If the service date moves beyond the validity period, a new request is required.
