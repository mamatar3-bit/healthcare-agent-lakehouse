# Telehealth Services Policy

Riverbend Health Network (fictional) — Ambulatory Policy AM-220. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Eligible visits
Established-patient follow-ups, medication management, behavioral health visits and post-discharge check-ins may be delivered by telehealth. New-patient visits requiring a physical examination, procedures, and visits for acute chest pain or shortness of breath must be in person.

## Consent and identity
The clinician confirms the patient's identity and location at the start of every telehealth visit and documents verbal consent. If the patient is located outside a state where the clinician is licensed, the visit must be rescheduled.

## Technology
Use only the network's approved video platform. Audio-only visits are allowed for behavioral health and when the patient cannot use video, and must be documented as audio-only.

## Billing
Telehealth visits are billed with the same evaluation and management codes as in-person visits (for example 99213 or 99214) with the payer-required place-of-service code and modifier. Medicaid managed care reimburses telehealth at parity with in-person visits.

## Emergencies
If a patient reports an emergency during a telehealth visit, the clinician instructs them to call 911 and stays on the line when possible.
