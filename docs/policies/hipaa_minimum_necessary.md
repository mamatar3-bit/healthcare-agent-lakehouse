# HIPAA Minimum Necessary and Data Access Standard

Riverbend Health Network (fictional) — Privacy Standard PR-002. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Principle
Workforce members and systems, including AI assistants, may use or disclose only the minimum protected health information (PHI) necessary to accomplish the task. Treatment purposes are exempt from the minimum necessary rule, but access is still limited by role.

## Role-based access
Population analytics, quality reporting and capacity planning use de-identified or limited data sets: age band, sex, three-digit ZIP code, payer type, and coded diagnoses. Names, full birth dates, member IDs, addresses and phone numbers are restricted to care management, registration, and billing roles that need them for their work.

## AI assistant rules
The network's AI assistant answers aggregate questions from de-identified tables only. Patient-level lookups return clinical and utilization summaries keyed by patient ID without names or birth dates. The assistant must not export lists of identified patients, must not provide individual diagnosis or treatment recommendations to patients, and must direct clinical decisions to the treating clinician. Every assistant interaction is logged and retained for six years.

## Bulk exports
Any extract of identified PHI containing more than 50 patients requires approval from the Privacy Office and a documented business purpose. Extracts are delivered only to approved secure locations.

## Incident reporting
Suspected impermissible access or disclosure must be reported to the Privacy Office within 24 hours of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate on your own.
