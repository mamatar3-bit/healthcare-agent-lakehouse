# Care Management Program Guide

Riverbend Health Network (fictional) — Population Health Program PH-150. Effective February 1, 2026.

## Eligibility
Patients are eligible for care management when they meet any of these criteria: LACE score of 10 or more at discharge, two or more inpatient admissions in 12 months, three or more emergency visits in six months, or two or more chronic conditions with an HbA1c above 9 percent in the last 6 months.

## Referral process
Hospitalists, primary care providers and the discharge team can refer through the care management order. Referrals from the readmission risk workflow are created automatically for high-risk discharges. A care manager contacts the patient within two business days of referral.

## Program components
Each enrolled patient has a care plan with goals, a named care manager, medication review by a pharmacist within 30 days, and a monthly check-in by phone or telehealth. Diabetes patients with HbA1c above 9 percent are offered diabetes education and a quarterly endocrinology review.

## Graduation
Patients graduate after 90 days without an emergency visit or admission and when care plan goals are met. Graduated patients receive a quarterly check-in for six months.

## Metrics
The program reports enrollment, engagement rate, 30-day readmissions among enrolled patients compared with eligible non-enrolled patients, and emergency visits per 1,000 enrolled patients.
