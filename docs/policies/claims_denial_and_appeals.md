# Claims Denial Management and Appeals

Riverbend Health Network (fictional) — Revenue Cycle Policy RC-305. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Purpose
Defines how denied claims are worked, prioritized, and appealed so that recoverable revenue is not lost to missed deadlines.

## Work queue prioritization
Denied claims are routed to the denial work queue the business day after the remittance posts. Work them in this order: claims within 15 days of the appeal deadline, then claims with billed amounts above $5,000, then all others oldest first.

## Common denial codes and first actions
- CO-197 (prior authorization absent): check whether an authorization exists but was not attached; if so, submit a corrected claim with the authorization number. If none exists and the service was urgent, request retroactive authorization.
- CO-16 (missing information): correct the claim and resubmit; this is not an appeal.
- CO-50 (not medically necessary): request the clinical notes and file a first-level appeal with a letter of medical necessity from the treating provider.
- CO-29 (timely filing expired): appeal only with proof of timely original submission, such as a clearinghouse acceptance report.
- CO-22 (coordination of benefits): verify primary and secondary coverage with the patient and rebill the correct payer.

## Appeal deadlines by payer type
Commercial plans allow 180 days from the denial date for a first-level appeal. Medicare Advantage plans allow 65 days for a request for reconsideration. Medicaid managed care plans allow 60 days. Deadlines are counted from the date on the remittance advice.

## Appeal package
Include the claim, the remittance advice, a cover letter stating the specific reason the denial is incorrect, supporting clinical documentation, and any authorization or eligibility evidence. Appeals for CO-50 denials must include a provider-signed letter of medical necessity.

## Escalation
If a first-level appeal is upheld and the amount exceeds $2,500, escalate to second-level appeal and notify the payer contracting manager. Patterns of more than 20 denials of the same type from one payer in a month are reported to the Revenue Integrity Committee.
