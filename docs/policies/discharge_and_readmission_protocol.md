# Discharge Planning and 30-Day Readmission Reduction Protocol

Riverbend Health Network (fictional) — Clinical Operations Protocol CO-410. Effective March 1, 2026.

## Purpose
Reduce avoidable 30-day all-cause readmissions through risk stratification at discharge and structured follow-up. The network measures readmissions as any unplanned inpatient admission within 30 days of a prior discharge, excluding patients who expired.

## Risk stratification using the LACE index
Every inpatient is scored with the LACE index before discharge: Length of stay, Acuity of admission, Comorbidities (Charlson score), and Emergency department visits in the prior six months. Scores range from 0 to 19. A score of 10 or more is high risk, 5 to 9 is moderate risk, and below 5 is low risk.

## Interventions by risk level
- High risk (LACE 10 or more): pharmacist medication reconciliation before discharge, a follow-up appointment scheduled within 7 days before the patient leaves, a care management referral, and a nurse phone call within 48 hours.
- Moderate risk (LACE 5 to 9): follow-up appointment within 14 days and a nurse phone call within 72 hours.
- Low risk (LACE below 5): standard discharge instructions and a primary care follow-up within 30 days.

## Heart failure and COPD bundles
Patients discharged with heart failure receive a scale, daily weight instructions, and a diuretic action plan; any weight gain of more than 3 pounds in a day or 5 pounds in a week triggers a call to the heart failure clinic. COPD patients receive an inhaler technique review and a written exacerbation action plan.

## Medication reconciliation
The discharging clinician reconciles home medications against the inpatient medication list. High-risk combinations such as anticoagulants with NSAIDs, or amiodarone with warfarin or simvastatin, require pharmacist review before discharge.

## Teach-back
Nurses use the teach-back method for discharge instructions and document it in the discharge note. Instructions are provided in the patient's preferred language.

## Measurement
Readmission rates are reported monthly by facility, payer type and diagnosis category. Index admissions discharged in the last 30 days are excluded from the rate until their outcome window closes.
